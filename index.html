Electron Configuration Lab

Build, arrange, and discover electrons: shells like 2-8-1, valence electrons, periodic patterns, and subshells like 1s² 2s² 2p⁶ 3s¹.

Learning goals

Essential question: How are electrons arranged in atoms, and how does their arrangement influence the properties of elements?

Today I will investigate how electrons are distributed among energy levels and orbitals. So I can write electron configurations, determine valence electrons, and identify elements from their electron arrangements.

I am successful when I can:
  1. Find the number of electrons in a neutral atom from its atomic number.
  2. Describe how electrons occupy principal energy levels.
  3. Write shell configurations for the first 20 elements.
  4. Find the number of electrons in each occupied shell.
  5. Identify valence electrons of main-group elements.
  6. Explain configuration patterns across periods and down groups.
  7. Identify elements from electron configurations.
  8. Write basic subshell configurations.
  9. Apply the Aufbau principle, Hund’s rule, and the Pauli exclusion principle to orbital diagrams.
  10. Relate electron arrangement to the organization of the periodic table.
